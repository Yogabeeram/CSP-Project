Before you upload
  • Create the subjects and topics in the app first. Upload does not create missing curriculum records.
  • Topic name and unit must match the selected subject.
  • Difficulty must be EASY, MEDIUM or HARD.
  • BloomLevel must use one of the six taxonomy values.
  • Duplicate question text for the same topic is skipped and reported.
